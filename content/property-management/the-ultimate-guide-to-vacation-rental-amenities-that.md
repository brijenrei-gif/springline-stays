## The Foundation: Non-Negotiable Amenities for Every STR

Before we dive into the "wow" factor items, let's cover the absolute must-haves. Missing any of these can be an immediate dealbreaker for potential guests and a surefire way to get a mediocre review. Think of these as the cost of entry in today's short-term rental market.

### Blazing-Fast, Reliable Wi-Fi
This is the new electricity. It's not a luxury; it's a utility. Guests expect to stream movies, join Zoom calls, and upload their vacation photos without a hitch. Don't cheap out on your internet plan. We recommend a minimum of 100 Mbps download speed, but 300+ is ideal, especially for larger homes. Mention the speed in your listing description—it’s a major selling point.

### Keyless, Contactless Entry
Guests love the convenience of a smart lock. It means no fumbling for keys, no stressful key handoffs, and no worries about losing a key during their stay. For owners, it's a massive security and operational win. You can issue unique codes for each guest, cleaner, and maintenance professional, and the codes automatically activate and expire with each reservation. It’s safer, more efficient, and what modern travelers expect.

### A Genuinely "Fully-Stocked" Kitchen
This is one of the most common areas where hosts fall short. "Fully-stocked" means more than just a few mismatched plates and a single frying pan. A truly well-equipped kitchen should include:
*   **Complete set of cookware:** Pots, pans, baking sheets, mixing bowls.
*   **Quality cooking utensils:** Spatulas, whisks, knives that are actually sharp.
*   **Ample dinnerware and silverware:** Enough for at least 1.5x the maximum occupancy.
*   **A solid coffee setup:** This is non-negotiable. Offer a drip coffee maker (with filters), a Keurig, or a French press. Provide a starter pack of coffee, sugar, and creamer.
*   **Essential appliances:** Microwave, toaster, and a blender are standard.
*   **The small things that matter:** Wine opener, can opener, cutting boards, salt, pepper, and cooking oil.

### Impeccable Cleanliness and High-Quality Linens
This should go without saying, but cleanliness is paramount. A single stray hair can derail a 5-star experience. Professional cleaning is a must. Beyond that, invest in your linens. This includes comfortable, high-quality mattresses, plush pillows, and soft, 100% cotton towels. Don’t use your old, mismatched towels from home. Providing a luxurious sleeping and bathing experience is one of the easiest ways to make a guest feel pampered.

## Level Up: Amenities That Create 5-Star Experiences

Once you have the foundation solid, it's time to add the amenities that make your property unforgettable. This is how you justify a higher price point and become a guest's first choice. The key is to think about *who* you're trying to attract.

<a name="family-fun"></a>
### For the Family Fun-Seekers

Families are often planning their entire trip around the rental. If you can provide built-in entertainment, you become the destination. This is one of the most effective ways **str amenities boost bookings** for larger properties.

![kids playing a board game with their parents in a cozy living room](/static/images/blog/property-management/kids-playing-a-board-game-with-their-parents-in-a-7d9091.jpg)

*   **Game Rooms:** A dedicated space with a pool table, foosball, air hockey, or classic arcade games is a massive draw. Add a smart TV with gaming consoles (like a Nintendo Switch or PS5) and you'll be a hero to parents and kids alike.
*   **Outdoor Entertainment:** Think beyond a simple grill. A fire pit with comfortable seating for s'mores nights is a huge win. For a bigger investment, consider amenities like a private hot tub, a mini-golf course, or a bocce ball court. These features create photo-worthy moments and lasting memories.
*   **Kid-Friendly Gear:** Make travel easier for parents by providing a high chair, a pack-n-play, and kids' dinnerware. It's a small touch that shows you understand their needs.

<a name="relaxation"></a>
### For the Relaxation Gurus

Some guests aren't looking for adventure; they're looking for an escape. They want to unwind, de-stress, and recharge. Catering to this crowd is all about creating a serene, spa-like atmosphere.

*   **Hot Tubs & Saunas:** This is arguably the number one amenity for boosting off-season bookings and increasing your average daily rate (ADR). The allure of a private hot tub, especially one with a view, is incredibly powerful.
*   **Outdoor Sanctuaries:** Create spaces designed for quiet enjoyment. This could be a comfortable hammock, a porch swing with plush cushions, or an elegant patio set with an umbrella. The goal is to create a spot where a guest can happily spend an hour reading a book.
*   **Spa-Like Bathrooms:** You don't need a full renovation. Simple upgrades like a rainfall showerhead, high-quality bath products (shampoo, conditioner, body wash), and a stack of fluffy, white towels can elevate the entire experience. Blackout curtains in the bedrooms are another must-have for this guest type.
*   **The Power of a View:** While not something you can add, a great view is a top-tier amenity. If you have one, make it the star of your listing. ### For the Digital Nomads & Bleisure Travelers

The line between work and travel has blurred. A growing number of guests need a functional space to be productive during their stay.

![stylish dedicated home office workspace with an ergonomic chair and large monitor](/static/images/blog/property-management/stylish-dedicated-home-office-workspace-with-an-er-979672.jpg)

*   **A Dedicated Workspace:** This means more than a wobbly desk crammed into a corner of the bedroom. A true dedicated workspace has a comfortable, ergonomic chair, good lighting, and easy access to power outlets.
*   **Tech-Friendly Touches:** Provide a monitor with an HDMI cable, a multi-port USB charging station on the nightstands, and ensure your Wi-Fi signal is strong in the workspace area.
*   **A High-End Coffee Station:** As mentioned before, coffee is key, but for this crowd, it's a productivity tool. Elevate your offering with a Nespresso machine, a coffee grinder and local beans, or an electric kettle with a selection of teas.

<a name="pets"></a>
### For the Four-Legged Family Members

"Pet-friendly" is one of the most-searched-for amenities on booking platforms. By welcoming pets, you dramatically expand your pool of potential guests. And these guests are often more responsible and willing to pay a premium.

*   **A Secure, Fenced Yard:** This is the holy grail for dog owners. It provides peace of mind and a safe place for their pet to play.
*   **Thoughtful Pet Amenities:** Go beyond just allowing them. Provide food and water bowls, a welcome treat, a durable dog bed, and waste bags. Keep a list of local pet-friendly parks, restaurants, and vet clinics handy.
*   **Durable, Pet-Friendly Furnishings:** Use leather or tightly woven fabrics that are easy to clean. Opt for hard-surface flooring over wall-to-wall carpet where possible. This protects your investment and makes cleaning easier for everyone.

## The Local Advantage: Amenities That Tell a Story

The best amenities connect your guests to the local area. This is how you transform your property from a generic rental into a curated, boutique experience. It shows you care and provides a level of hospitality that self-managed properties often miss.

*   **Local Welcome Basket:** Partner with local businesses. Leave a bag of coffee from a nearby roaster, a craft beer from a local brewery, or snacks from a neighborhood bakery. It’s a fantastic way to make a first impression.
*   **The Ultimate Guidebook:** Create your own digital or physical guidebook that goes beyond the tourist traps. Share your favorite hidden-gem restaurant, the best hiking trail that isn't crowded, or where to get the best ice cream. This insider knowledge is priceless.
*   **Location-Specific Gear:** Make it easy for guests to enjoy the best of your area.
    *   **Beach Property?** Provide beach chairs, an umbrella, a cooler, and sand toys.
    *   **Mountain Cabin?** Offer hiking poles, a local trail map, and a pair of binoculars.
    *   **City Apartment?** Include a couple of reloadable transit cards or a list of the best happy hour spots within walking distance.

<a name="the-bottom-line"></a>
## The Bottom Line: Calculating the ROI of Your Amenities

Remember, every amenity is an investment. Before you splurge on a six-person sauna, do the math. A hot tub might cost $8,000, but if it allows you to increase your nightly rate by $50 and boosts your occupancy by 20%, it can pay for itself in less than a year.

Analyze top-performing properties in your area. What do they offer? Use tools like AirDNA or PriceLabs to see the revenue difference between properties with and without premium amenities like pools or hot tubs. Start with the investments that have the broadest appeal and the highest proven ROI. Often, the path to **increase str revenue** is paved with thoughtful, guest-centric amenities.

<a name="faq"></a>
## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the single most important amenity for a vacation rental?**
Without a doubt, fast and reliable Wi-Fi is the most critical amenity in today's market. It's a non-negotiable for nearly every type of traveler, from families wanting to stream movies to professionals needing to work remotely.

**Do hot tubs really increase STR revenue?**
Yes, overwhelmingly so. A hot tub is one of the top revenue-driving amenities you can add. It not only allows you to charge a higher nightly rate but also significantly boosts your occupancy, particularly during the shoulder seasons and winter months when outdoor activities are limited.

**How much should I spend on amenities for my short-term rental?**
There's no magic number, as it depends on your market and target nightly rate. A good rule of thumb is to start by ensuring your property has all the "Foundation" amenities covered perfectly. Then, reinvest a percentage of your profits each year into one or two "Level Up" amenities that align with your target guest profile.

**What are some low-cost amenities that make a big impact?**
You don't have to break the bank to impress guests. Some high-impact, low-cost amenities include a welcome basket with local treats, a quality coffee station, a Bluetooth speaker, board games and puzzles, and providing chargers for popular phone models. These thoughtful touches show you care and can be the difference in a 5-star review.
