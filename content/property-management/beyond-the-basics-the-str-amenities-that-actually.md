## The Foundation: Nailing the Non-Negotiable Basics

Before we dive into the exciting, premium amenities, let's cover the essentials. These are the items that guests now consider standard. Lacking any of these can lead to negative reviews and make your property feel dated. Think of these as the price of entry into the competitive STR market.

### High-Speed, Reliable Wi-Fi
In 2024, fast and free Wi-Fi is as essential as running water. Guests rely on it for everything from streaming movies after a day of exploring to logging into crucial work meetings. A slow or spotty connection is one of the fastest ways to get a bad review.

*   **Pro Tip:** Invest in a mesh Wi-Fi system for larger homes to ensure strong coverage in every room, from the basement game room to the upstairs bedrooms. Include the Wi-Fi speed (e.g., "Blazing-fast 500 Mbps Wi-Fi") in your listing description.

### A Fully-Stocked Kitchen
Even if guests plan to eat out, they want the *option* to cook. A "fully-stocked kitchen" means more than just plates and forks.

*   **Must-Haves:** Quality cookware (pots, pans), bakeware, ample utensils, a full knife block, cutting boards, mixing bowls, and a coffee maker (offer both a drip machine and a Keurig or Nespresso if possible).
*   **Go the Extra Mile:** Provide salt, pepper, basic spices, and cooking oil. These small touches prevent guests from having to buy full-sized containers for a short stay and are always appreciated.

![modern, well-stocked vacation rental kitchen with coffee station](/static/images/blog/property-management/modern-well-stocked-vacation-rental-kitchen-with-c-a19211.jpg)

### Smart TV & Streaming Services
Guests expect to be able to log into their Netflix, Hulu, or Disney+ accounts. A Smart TV is the easiest way to provide this. If you don't have a Smart TV, a simple streaming device like a Roku or Amazon Fire Stick is an affordable alternative.

### Keyless, Contactless Entry
Fumbling with lockboxes in the dark or coordinating key handoffs is a thing of the past. A smart lock with a keypad offers numerous benefits:
*   **Guest Convenience:** Guests can check in anytime after the designated time without needing to meet anyone.
*   **Security:** You can assign a unique code for each guest that is only active during their stay, eliminating the risk of lost or copied keys.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** No more calls about lost keys or lockouts. It streamlines the entire check-in process for you and your team.

### The "Wow" Factor: Experiential & Entertainment Amenities

These amenities transform your property from just a rental into a destination in itself. They are particularly effective for attracting large groups and families who are looking for built-in entertainment.

It adds a touch of luxury and is a major draw for guests year-round, whether it's for a soothing soak after a day of hiking or a warm dip on a cool evening. A sauna provides a similar wellness-focused, high-end appeal.

#### Game Rooms & Themed Spaces
A dedicated game room is a guaranteed hit with families and groups of friends. You don't need a massive arcade; even a well-outfitted corner can do the trick.
*   **Popular Options:** Pool table, foosball, air hockey, a retro arcade machine (like Pac-Man or a multi-cade), or a dartboard.
*   **Don't Forget Low-Tech:** A great selection of board games, card games, and puzzles is an easy and affordable way to cater to all ages.

#### Fire Pits
A fire pit creates a natural gathering spot for guests to unwind, roast s'mores, and share stories. It extends the usable living space to the outdoors and adds immense value, especially in locations with beautiful scenery or clear, starry nights.

*   **Pro Tip:** Provide a starter set of firewood, lighters, and skewers for marshmallows. This small gesture removes any friction for the guest and lets them enjoy the amenity from their very first night.

### The Great Outdoors: Curating an Unforgettable Al Fresco Experience

Guests book vacation rentals to enjoy the location, so make sure your outdoor space is as comfortable and inviting as your indoor space.

#### BBQ & Outdoor Dining
A high-quality grill (gas is often easier for guests) and a comfortable outdoor dining table are essential, especially for properties that can host larger groups. This encourages guests to cook at the property, saving them money and allowing them to spend more quality time together.

### Catering to the Modern Traveler: Work-from-Anywhere Setups

The rise of remote work has created a new class of traveler: the digital nomad or "flexcationer" who blends work and leisure. Catering to this demographic can open up your property to longer, mid-week bookings.

*   **Dedicated Workspace:** This doesn't have to be a separate office. A simple desk and a comfortable, ergonomic chair in a quiet corner of a bedroom or living room is sufficient. Avoid making guests work from the dining table.
*   **Essential Tech:** Include a power strip with USB ports, good task lighting, and if possible, an external monitor with an HDMI cable. These are small additions that make a huge difference for someone working remotely.
*   **Highlight It:** Create a "Dedicated Workspace" section in your listing and include a clear photo. This will help you show up in searches for travelers specifically looking for work-friendly properties.

### The Untapped Goldmine: Becoming Pet-Friendly

Millions of people travel with their pets every year, yet the number of high-quality, pet-friendly rentals is still surprisingly low. By welcoming pets, you tap into a massive, underserved market of travelers who are often more responsible and willing to pay a premium.

*   **The Financials:** Most pet-friendly hosts charge a pet fee (e.g., $50-$150 per stay) which can easily cover any additional cleaning and adds a significant boost to your revenue.
*   **The "Stickiness":** Once a guest finds a great pet-friendly rental, they are highly likely to become a repeat booker, as the search for another suitable property can be a hassle.
*   **Best Practices:** Don't just *allow* pets; *welcome* them. Provide durable food and water bowls, a welcome treat, a few waste bags, and maybe an old towel for wiping paws. ## The Smart Investment: How Amenities Directly Impact Your Bottom Line

It’s crucial to view amenities not as costs, but as strategic investments that directly **increase STR revenue**. Here’s how:

1.  **Higher Average Daily Rate (ADR):** Properties with premium amenities like hot tubs, game rooms, or fire pits can command a 15-30% higher nightly rate than comparable properties without them.
2.  **Increased Occupancy:** Unique and desirable amenities make your listing stand out, leading to more clicks and more bookings. They can also help you fill your calendar during the slower shoulder seasons.
3.  **Better Reviews:** Thoughtful amenities lead to happier guests. Happier guests leave 5-star reviews, which in turn boosts your listing's visibility and search ranking, creating a powerful cycle of success.
4.  **Competitive Advantage:** In a crowded market, a unique set of amenities can be the single deciding factor that makes a guest choose your property over a competitor's.

The key is to choose amenities that fit your property, your location, and your ideal guest profile. A hot tub is perfect for a mountain cabin, while high-end beach chairs and an outdoor shower are must-haves for a coastal condo.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most requested vacation rental amenity?
While it can vary by location, a private hot tub is consistently one of the most searched-for and requested amenities by vacation rental guests. It adds a layer of luxury and relaxation that appeals to a wide range of travelers, from couples on a romantic getaway to families looking to unwind.

### How can I increase my vacation rental bookings?
One of the most effective ways to increase bookings is by investing in strategic amenities that make your property stand out. Features like hot tubs, fire pits, game rooms, and pet-friendly policies can significantly boost your listing's visibility and appeal. Additionally, professional photography, dynamic pricing, and excellent guest communication are crucial to maximizing your occupancy.

### Are pet-friendly rentals more profitable?
Yes, pet-friendly rentals can be significantly more profitable. Owners can typically charge a pet fee per stay, which directly increases revenue. Furthermore, by catering to the large market of pet owners, you can increase your overall occupancy rate, especially during off-peak seasons, as pet owners often have more flexibility in their travel dates.

### Is a hot tub worth it for a short-term rental?
For most properties, especially those in leisure destinations (mountains, coast, etc.), a hot tub is an excellent investment with a high ROI. It allows you to increase your nightly rate, boosts your occupancy, and is a major differentiating factor that helps your property get booked over competitors. The key is to factor in the ongoing costs of professional maintenance and cleaning to ensure it's always safe and pristine for guests.
