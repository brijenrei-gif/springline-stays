Everyone comes to Panama City Beach for the 27 miles of sugar-white sand, and for good reason. It’s legendary. But what if I told you that some of the most memorable moments on your trip will happen just a few steps away from the shoreline?

Locals know that the real magic of PCB is its incredible variety. One day you can be kayaking next to a dolphin in a pristine coastal lagoon, and the next you're exploring lush forest trails or cheering at a dueling piano bar. The beach is the main event, but these are the unforgettable opening acts and encores.

Forget the generic tourist traps. This is your guide to the other side of Panama City Beach—the adventures, flavors, and wild spaces that make this place so much more than just a pretty coastline.

## <a name="1-get-lost-in-the-wilds-of-st-andrews-state-park"></a>1. Get Lost in the Wilds of St. Andrews State Park

Yes, St. Andrews State Park has over a mile of its own gorgeous beaches, but sticking only to the sand here is a rookie mistake. This 1,200-acre former military reservation is a paradise for nature lovers.

**The Local Tip:** Skip the crowded main beach entrance on a busy Saturday and head straight for the jetties. It’s one of the best spots for snorkeling in the entire Panhandle. You’ll see sheepshead, spadefish, and tons of other colorful marine life darting among the rocks. On the other side of the park, rent a kayak from the camp store and paddle around the Grand Lagoon. It’s much calmer than the Gulf and you have a high chance of spotting dolphins hunting for fish. Also, don't miss the short hike to Gator Lake to see, you guessed it, alligators sunning themselves (from a safe distance, of course).

*   **Address**: [4607 State Park Ln, Panama City, FL 32408](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4607+State+Park+Ln+Panama+City+FL+32408)
*   **Best For**: Nature lovers, snorkelers, families looking for calm waters.



## <a name="2-hike-or-bike-through-a-2900-acre-oasis"></a>2. Hike or Bike Through a 2,900-Acre Oasis

Need a break from the sun and salt? Head inland to the Panama City Beach Conservation Park. This massive tract of protected wetlands and pine forest is crisscrossed with over 24 miles of trails and boardwalks, perfect for a morning hike or bike ride.

The trails are flat, well-marked, and range from a short half-mile loop to a challenging 11-mile trek. It's a fantastic spot for birdwatching—keep an eye out for eagles, ospreys, and various wading birds. You might even spot a white-tailed deer or a fox if you go early in the morning.

**The Local Tip:** Pack bug spray, especially in the summer. The one-mile-long Cypress Pond Trail is a must-do; its elevated boardwalk takes you right over a scenic swampy area that feels a world away from the beach.

*   **Address**: [100 Conservation Dr, Panama City Beach, FL 32413](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=100+Conservation+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32413)
*   **Best For**: Hikers, cyclists, birdwatchers, and anyone needing a dose of green.

## <a name="3-have-a-real-conversation-with-a-dolphin-at-gulf-world"></a>3. Have a Real Conversation with a Dolphin at Gulf World Marine Park

Gulf World is a PCB institution, but it's much more than just a place to watch dolphin and sea lion shows (though those are great, too). The real value here is in the up-close, interactive experiences.

You can sign up for a "Meet & Greet" with a sea lion, get in the water for a "Royal Swim" with dolphins, or even snorkel with stingrays. These programs are incredibly educational and give you a new appreciation for these amazing animals. It’s a splurge, but the memory of a dolphin playfully nudging your hand is something you'll talk about for years.

**The Local Tip:** Book your interactive program well in advance, especially during peak season, as they sell out fast. The general admission ticket gets you into all the daily shows, so plan to spend at least half a day here to get the full experience.

*   **Address**: [15412 Front Beach Rd, Panama City Beach, FL 32413](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=15412+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32413)
*   **Best For**: Animal lovers of all ages, families looking for an unforgettable experience.

## <a name="4-make-pier-park-your-all-day-destination"></a>4. Make Pier Park Your All-Day Destination

Pier Park is the undisputed heart of Panama City Beach's dining, shopping, and entertainment scene. This massive outdoor complex is so much more than a mall—it’s a destination in itself. You can find everything from big-name stores like Target and Dillard's to quirky local boutiques.

### What to Do There:
*   **Shop 'Til You Drop:** Spend hours browsing Ron Jon Surf Shop, pick up some souvenirs at a beachy boutique, or grab a new book at Books-A-Million.
*   **Eat Your Heart Out:** The options are endless. My personal favorite move is to grab a gourmet burger and a craft beer at The Craft Bar, followed by a ridiculously good milkshake from The Yard Milkshake Bar. For something a bit more upscale with a view, check out Pompano Joe's right across the street.
*   **Get Entertained:** Ride the iconic SkyWheel for breathtaking views of the coastline, catch a movie at the Grand IMAX theatre, or challenge your family at the Mirror Maze & Laser Craze.

**The Local Tip:** Park in the lots behind the main strip (near the movie theater) to find easier parking. In the evening, the area comes alive with live music, street performers, and a fun, festive atmosphere.



## <a name="5-discover-the-murals-of-historic-st-andrews"></a>5. Discover the Murals of Historic St. Andrews

Just a short drive over the Hathaway Bridge, you'll find the charming, historic neighborhood of St. Andrews. It has a completely different vibe from the high-rises of PCB—think moss-draped oaks, historic homes, and a funky, artistic soul.

The best way to explore is by foot on a self-guided mural tour. The "Murals of St. Andrews" project has decorated the town with vibrant, large-scale paintings depicting the area's rich history and culture. Grab a coffee from a local cafe like Amavida and just wander. It's a perfect, low-key afternoon activity that reveals a side of the area most tourists miss.

*   **Address**: Start your walk near [1201 Beck Ave, Panama City, FL 32401](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1201+Beck+Ave+Panama+City+FL+32401) (St. Andrews Marina)
*   **Best For**: Art lovers, history buffs, and those seeking a quieter, more authentic local scene.

## <a name="6-find-your-new-favorite-beer-at-a-local-brewery"></a>6. Find Your New Favorite Beer at a Local Brewery

The craft beer scene here is thriving. After a long day in the sun, there's nothing better than cooling off with a locally brewed pint. Two standouts are Idyll Hounds Brewing Company and Salty Oak Brewing Co.

Idyll Hounds, located a bit inland, has a fantastic taproom and a wide variety of creative brews. Salty Oak, in St. Andrews, is smaller and has a super chill, dog-friendly vibe. Both offer a taste of the local flavor that you won't find in a can of national-brand light beer.

**The Local Tip:** Check their social media pages before you go. They often have food trucks on-site, especially on weekends, making it a one-stop shop for dinner and drinks.

*   **Idyll Hounds Address**: [845 Serenoa Rd, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=845+Serenoa+Rd+Santa+Rosa+Beach+FL+32459) (*Note: a bit of a drive, but worth it for beer aficionados*)
*   **Salty Oak Address**: [2337 St Andrews Blvd, Panama City, FL 32405](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2337+St+Andrews+Blvd+Panama+City+FL+32405)
*   **Best For**: Adults, craft beer enthusiasts.

## <a name="7-catch-the-big-one-from-the-russell-fields-pier"></a>7. Catch 'The Big One' from the Russell-Fields Pier

Stretching an incredible 1,500 feet into the Gulf of Mexico, the Russell-Fields Pier (also known as the City Pier) is an angler's dream and a sightseer's delight. You don't need a fishing license to fish from the pier; your admission fee covers it.

You can rent gear and buy bait right there at the entrance. Depending on the season, you can catch everything from Spanish mackerel and cobia to king mackerel and flounder. Even if you're not fishing, it's worth the small fee to walk out to the end for an unparalleled view of the coastline. It's one of the best places to watch the sunset.

**The Local Tip:** The pier is located directly across from Pier Park, making it a perfect pre-dinner or post-shopping activity. The best fishing is often at sunrise or sunset when the fish are most active.

*   **Address**: [16201 Front Beach Rd, Panama City Beach, FL 32413](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=16201+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32413)
*   **Best For**: Fishing, sunset views, photography.

## <a name="8-see-the-emerald-coast-from-the-sky"></a>8. See the Emerald Coast from the Sky

Want a truly unforgettable view? See Panama City Beach from a helicopter. Several companies offer tours that take you soaring over the coastline. You'll see the piers, the state parks, and the endless stretch of emerald water from a perspective that's simply breathtaking.

It's an incredible way to spot marine life, too. From above, you can often see huge schools of fish, sea turtles, and even sharks cruising just offshore. It's a quick but exhilarating adventure that will give you a whole new appreciation for the scale and beauty of the area.

*   **Operator to Check Out**: Panhandle Helicopter
*   **Best For**: Thrill-seekers, photographers, special occasions.

## <a name="9-unleash-your-inner-kid-at-race-city"></a>9. Unleash Your Inner Kid at Race City

Sometimes you just need some good, old-fashioned, non-beach fun. Race City is the place for that. It’s home to the fastest, tallest, and steepest go-kart tracks in Panama City Beach, including the four-story "Hurricane" track.

Beyond the go-karts, there's a huge indoor arcade, mini-golf, and thrill rides like the SkyCoaster. It’s pure, unapologetic family fun and a perfect way to burn off some energy, especially on a day when the weather might not be perfect for the beach.

*   **Address**: [9523 Front Beach Rd, Panama City Beach, FL 32407](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=9523+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32407)
*   **Best For**: Families with kids and teens, competitive friends.

## <a name="10-paddleboard-on-a-rare-coastal-dune-lake"></a>10. Paddleboard on a Rare Coastal Dune Lake

Just on the west end of PCB lies Lake Powell, one of Florida's rare and precious coastal dune lakes. These unique ecosystems, found in only a handful of places worldwide, are bodies of fresh water that periodically open and exchange water with the salt water of the Gulf.

The result is a calm, brackish lake that's perfect for paddleboarding and kayaking, surrounded by the natural beauty of Camp Helen State Park. You can rent boards nearby and spend a peaceful morning gliding across the water, spotting fish and birds, without worrying about waves or currents.

*   **Access Point**: Camp Helen State Park, [23937 Panama City Beach Pkwy, Panama City Beach, FL 32413](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=23937+Panama+City+Beach+Pkwy+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32413)
*   **Best For**: Kayaking, paddleboarding, peaceful nature escapes.



## <a name="11-hunt-for-treasure-at-the-zoogallery"></a>11. Hunt for Treasure at the ZooGallery

This isn't your average souvenir shop. The ZooGallery is a whimsical and wonderful maze of art, jewelry, funky furniture, and unique gifts. It's been a Panhandle landmark for decades for a reason.

Walking through the store feels like an adventure in itself. You'll find everything from beautiful, handcrafted jewelry to hilarious t-shirts and bizarre sculptures. It's the perfect place to find a truly one-of-a-kind memento from your trip that isn't a cheap plastic keychain.

*   **Address**: [1201 Beck Ave, Panama City, FL 32401](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1201+Beck+Ave+Panama+City+FL+32401)
*   **Best For**: Unique gifts, art lovers, rainy day browsing.

## <a name="panama-city-beach-faq"></a>Panama City Beach FAQ

**Q: What is there to do in Panama City Beach besides the beach?**
A: Plenty! You can explore nature at St. Andrews State Park and the Conservation Park, shop and dine at Pier Park, visit Gulf World Marine Park, discover local art in Historic St. Andrews, go fishing on the Russell-Fields Pier, or enjoy family fun centers like Race City.

**Q: Is Panama City Beach walkable?**
A: Certain areas, like Pier Park and the surrounding blocks, are very walkable. However, to get between major attractions like St. Andrews State Park and the west end of the beach, you will need a car, bike, or to use the local trolley service.

**Q: What is the best month to visit Panama City Beach?**
A: The best time to visit depends on your priorities. For the best weather and warm water, May through October is ideal, though it's also the busiest. For fewer crowds and lower prices, consider the shoulder seasons of April or October. The weather is still beautiful, but the summer crowds have thinned out.

**Q: Can you see dolphins in Panama City Beach?**
A: Absolutely! Dolphins are frequently spotted from the shore, piers, and boats. For the best chance of an up-close encounter, take a dedicated dolphin-watching tour or kayak in the Grand Lagoon area of St. Andrews State Park.
