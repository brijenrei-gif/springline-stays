Once you've claimed your rooms and slathered on some sunscreen, do a quick grocery run. The Publix at Breakfast Point ([11240 Panama City Beach Pkwy](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=11240+Panama+City+Beach+Pkwy+Panama+City+Beach+FL)) is usually a good bet. Grab essentials: coffee, breakfast items, snacks for the beach, and your favorite drinks. Having these on hand saves you time and money.

### Late Afternoon: The Main Event

Now, the moment you've been waiting for. Grab your towels and head down to the shore. The sand here is unique—it's made of 99% quartz crystal, which is why it's so brilliantly white and squeaks under your feet. Walk along the shoreline, let the emerald water wash over your ankles, and just breathe. You're officially on beach time.

### Evening: A Classic PCB Sunset Dinner

For your first night, you don't need anything fancy. You need a place with a great view, a casual vibe, and good food. Head to **Schooners Last Local Beach Club** ([5121 Gulf Dr](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=5121+Gulf+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL)).

**Local Tip:** Schooners is famous for its sunset cannon blast. They fire off a small cannon every single evening at sunset to celebrate the end of another day in paradise. Try to time your arrival to be seated about 45 minutes before sunset to catch the show. The Seared Crab Cakes are a classic, but don't sleep on the Tuna Medallions.

## Day 2: Pier Park Immersion and a Sunset Spectacle

Today is about exploring the modern heart of Panama City Beach. Pier Park is more than just a mall; it's an open-air destination with shopping, dining, and entertainment, all with a view of the Gulf.

### Morning: Retail Therapy and Local Finds

Get to Pier Park before the peak afternoon heat. The main strip, Pier Park Drive, is a colorful, walkable area filled with everything from big-name brands to quirky local boutiques.
- **Ron Jon Surf Shop:** A mandatory stop for beachwear, souvenirs, and soaking up the surf culture.
- **Coastal Casuals:** A great spot for high-quality, stylish beach apparel that doesn't scream "tourist."



Don't just stick to the main drag. The surrounding area has a Target and Walmart for any essentials you forgot, plus a movie theater and other entertainment options.

### Lunch: A Quick & Delicious Bite

You've got a ton of options in Pier Park. For something quick, delicious, and a local favorite, head to **The Yard Milkshake Bar**. It's pure indulgence, but you're on vacation. If you need something more substantial, **Pompano Joe's Seafood House** offers great food with a killer view right across the street. My go-to is the Voodoo Shrimp.

### Afternoon: Meet the Marine Life

Just a short drive or a healthy walk from Pier Park is **Gulf World Marine Park** ([15412 Front Beach Rd](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=15412+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). It's a fantastic, manageable-sized park where you can see dolphin shows, sea lion presentations, and learn about local marine conservation. The indoor exhibits are a great way to escape the afternoon sun for a bit. The stingray petting pool is always a huge hit.

### Evening: Walk the Pier

As the day cools down, it's time for one of PCB's most iconic experiences. Head to the **Russell-Fields Pier** ([16201 Front Beach Rd](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=16201+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL)), located directly across from Pier Park. It costs a few dollars to walk out on it, but it's worth every penny.

Strolling 1,500 feet out over the Gulf of Mexico gives you an entirely new perspective of the coastline. You'll see anglers reeling in their catches, pelicans dive-bombing for fish, and often, schools of dolphins or sea turtles cruising by. The absolute best time to go is for sunset. The view of the sun dipping below the horizon from the end of the pier is breathtaking.

For dinner, stay in the area. **The Back Porch Seafood & Oyster House** has a prime location and consistently good food. Or, for something more low-key, grab a pizza from **Marco's Pizza** and take it back to your condo's balcony.

## Day 3: The Wilds of St. Andrews and Shell Island

After a day of commercial fun, it's time to experience the raw, natural beauty that makes this part of Florida so special. Today is an adventure day, so pack sunscreen, water, and snacks.

### Morning: Exploring St. Andrews State Park

Drive east to **St. Andrews State Park** ([4607 State Park Ln](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4607+State+Park+Ln+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). This is consistently ranked as one of the best state parks in the country, and for good reason. It offers a glimpse of what the entire coastline looked like before development.

Your first stop inside the park should be the jetties. This is a fantastic spot for snorkeling (rent gear in town beforehand). The calm, protected waters are teeming with fish. It's also a great place to just watch the boats come and go through the pass. After the jetties, explore some of the park's nature trails. The Gator Lake trail gives you a chance to see alligators and wading birds in a safe, natural habitat.



### Afternoon: The Unspoiled Paradise of Shell Island

Inside St. Andrews State Park, you'll find the **Shell Island Shuttle**. This is the easiest way to get to the famous seven-mile-long, undeveloped barrier island.

**Pro Tip:** This is a truly wild experience. There are no restrooms, no concession stands, and no trash cans on Shell Island. You must pack in everything you need and pack out *everything* you bring.

The shuttle drops you on the calm, bay side of the island. A short walk over the dunes brings you to the pristine, untouched Gulf side. The shelling here is incredible, especially after a storm. Spend the afternoon swimming, searching for sand dollars, and enjoying the feeling of being on a deserted island. It’s a completely different vibe from the main beach and a must-do for any Panama City Beach travel guide.

### Evening: Casual Eats and Relaxation

After a long day in the sun, you'll be tired and hungry. Head back toward your rental and stop at **Patches Pub & Grill** ([4723 Thomas Dr](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4723+Thomas+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). It's a beloved local dive with surprisingly fantastic pizza and ice-cold beer. It's the perfect no-frills end to an adventurous day. Or, better yet, use that kitchen in your vacation rental. A simple pasta dinner on the balcony listening to the waves is sometimes the best meal of the trip.

## Day 4: Ecotourism Escape and West End Wonders

Let's explore the quieter, more natural side of PCB. The west end of the beach and the inland parks offer a peaceful contrast to the bustling center of town.

### Morning: Hike or Bike the Conservation Park

Head inland to the **Panama City Beach Conservation Park** ([100 Conservation Dr](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=100+Conservation+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). This massive, 2,900-acre park is a hidden gem. It features over 24 miles of trails that wind through cypress domes and pine forests.

You can choose your own adventure, from a simple one-mile stroll on a boardwalk to a more strenuous 11-mile loop. It's a fantastic spot for birdwatching, and if you go early in the morning, you might spot deer or even an alligator. Renting bikes is a great way to cover more ground. It's one of the best "off-the-beaten-path" things to do in Panama City Beach.

### Lunch: A Taste of Old Florida

After your hike, drive to the far west end of the beach. This area feels like a different world—less high-rises, more classic beach cottages. For lunch, stop at the iconic **Thomas Donut & Snack Shop** ([19208 Front Beach Rd](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=19208+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). Yes, they have amazing donuts (the red velvet is legendary), but their lunch menu is a local secret. Grab one of their simple, perfect sandwiches or a hot dog and enjoy it on the beach across the street.

### Afternoon: Relax and Recharge

The afternoon is for pure, unscheduled relaxation. Find a public beach access on the quiet west end and just chill. The crowds are thinner here, and the vibe is much more laid-back. Or, head back to your rental. A day like this is why booking a great place to stay is so important. A comfortable couch, a cool drink, and that stunning ocean view from your balcony is an activity in itself.

## Panama City Beach Itinerary FAQ

**Q: What is the best month to visit Panama City Beach?**
A: For the best balance of great weather and manageable crowds, the shoulder seasons are ideal. April, May, September, and October offer warm, sunny days, warm Gulf water, and lower prices than the peak summer months.

**Q: Is 5 days enough for Panama City Beach?**
A: Five days is a perfect amount of time for a first visit. It allows you to experience the main attractions like the beach and Pier Park, have an adventure day at St. Andrews or Shell Island, and still have plenty of time for relaxation without feeling rushed.

**Q: What is there to do in Panama City Beach besides the beach?**
A: Plenty! You can explore the trail systems at the Conservation Park, shop and dine at Pier Park, visit Gulf World Marine Park, go fishing or snorkeling at St. Andrews State Park, or take a boat tour to spot dolphins. The area is rich in both natural and man-made attractions.

**Q: Can you drink alcohol on Panama City Beach?**
A: Rules can change, but generally, alcohol is permitted on the beach for those 21 and over, EXCEPT during the month of March due to Spring Break regulations. Glass containers are never allowed. Always drink responsibly and clean up after yourself.
