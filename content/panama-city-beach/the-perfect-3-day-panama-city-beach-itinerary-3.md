## Day 1: Coastal Bliss & West End Wonders

Your first day is all about easing into vacation mode. It’s about soaking in the views, getting your bearings, and indulging in some of the best that the popular west end of PCB has to offer.

### Afternoon: Pier Park Exploration

Once you’ve had your fill of sun, it’s time to explore the heart of PCB’s modern entertainment district: Pier Park. This massive outdoor complex is more than just a shopping mall; it's a destination.

**Address:** [600 Pier Park Dr, Panama City Beach, FL 32413](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=600+Pier+Park+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32413)

**Local Tip:** Don't fight for parking right at the front. The lots behind the main strip (on the north side of the road, near the department stores) usually have plenty of space, and it's a pleasant walk through the complex.

Before you start shopping, grab a late lunch. Avoid the big chains and head to **Fatty's Sandwich Shop** ([9566 S Thomas Dr](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=9566+S+Thomas+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). It's a short 5-minute drive from the main Pier Park area but is a local institution. The sandwiches are huge, fresh, and consistently amazing. The "Fatty's Famous" is my go-to, but you can't go wrong.

Back at Pier Park, wander through the shops, grab a daiquiri from a walk-up bar, and maybe catch a movie at the Grand IMAX theatre. For the best view in town, take a ride on the **SkyWheel**. This 200-foot-tall observation wheel offers breathtaking panoramic views of the coastline and the pier. Go about an hour before sunset for the most spectacular lighting.



### Evening: Sunset Drinks & Upscale Dinner

As the sun begins to dip, walk across the street from Pier Park to the **Russell-Fields Pier**. It costs a few dollars to walk out onto it, but it's worth every penny for the sunset views and the chance to watch fishermen reel in their catches.

For a pre-dinner drink with your toes in the sand, head right next door to **Hook'd Pier Bar & Grill** ([16201 Front Beach Rd](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=16201+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). It's casual, fun, and has a prime location for watching the sky turn pink and orange.

For dinner, elevate the evening at **Firefly** ([535 N Richard Jackson Blvd](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=535+N+Richard+Jackson+Blvd+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). Tucked away in a quiet shopping center, walking into Firefly feels like being transported. The main dining room is centered around a huge, illuminated tree, creating a magical ambiance. It's known for its incredible steaks and she-crab soup, but the real showstopper is the sushi. It's some of the freshest and most creative in town. Reservations are highly recommended.

## Day 2: Wild Encounters & Local Flavors

Today, we trade the bustling pier for encounters with Florida's amazing wildlife—both in the water and on land. It’s a day for adventure, followed by a casual, satisfying meal.

### Morning: Meet the Marine Life

Get up close and personal with dolphins, sea lions, and more at **Gulf World Marine Park**. This is a PCB classic for a reason. It's smaller and more intimate than the massive theme parks in Orlando, allowing for a more personal experience.

**Address:** [15412 Front Beach Rd, Panama City Beach, FL 32413](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=15412+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32413)

The dolphin and sea lion shows are fantastic, but the real magic is in the interactive programs. You can book experiences to swim with dolphins, pet a stingray, or get a sloppy kiss from a sea lion.

**Insider Tip:** The "Meet & Greet" programs are a great value if you don't want to commit to the full swim experience. You still get to interact with the animals and learn from the trainers. Book these online well in advance, as they sell out, especially during peak season.

### Lunch: Quick & Delicious Tacos

After a morning at Gulf World, you'll be ready for a quick and satisfying lunch. Just down the road is **Diego's Burrito Factory & Margarita Bar** ([15514 Front Beach Rd](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=15514+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL)). It's a fast-casual spot where you build your own burritos, bowls, and tacos. The ingredients are always fresh, and the queso is dangerously good. Don't skip the "Screamin' Green" sauce if you like a little kick.

### Afternoon: Hike the Conservation Park

Ready to see a different side of Florida? Head inland to the **Panama City Beach Conservation Park**. This 2,900-acre wilderness preserve is a world away from the beachfront condos. It's a haven for birdwatchers, hikers, and anyone looking for a peaceful escape.

**Address:** [100 Conservation Dr, Panama City Beach, FL 32413](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=100+Conservation+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL+32413)

The park features over 24 miles of trails, ranging from short, easy loops to longer, more challenging paths. The trails are a mix of boardwalks over wetlands and sandy paths through pine forests.

*   **For a quick visit:** The 1-mile Cypress Pond Trail is a beautiful, shaded walk on a boardwalk, perfect for families.
*   **For a longer hike:** The 11-mile Gayle's Trails system is paved and popular with cyclists and runners.

Keep your eyes peeled for wildlife! It's common to see alligators (from a safe distance on the boardwalks), deer, snakes, and dozens of species of birds. Remember to bring water, sunscreen, and bug spray.

![peaceful wooden boardwalk trail through a Florida cypress swamp](/static/images/blog/panama-city-beach/peaceful-wooden-boardwalk-trail-through-a-florida-633671.jpg)

### Evening: Craft Beer & Casual Eats

After a day of adventure, unwind with some local craft beer and hearty food. **Props Craft Brewery & Grill** ([15900 Front Beach Rd](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=15900+Front+Beach+Rd+Panama+City+Beach+FL)) is an excellent choice. With an aviation theme and a solid lineup of house-brewed beers, it's a favorite among locals and visitors alike.

The Prop-A-Roni pizza is fantastic, and their burgers are top-notch. For beer, you can't go wrong with the Blonde Bomber Ale for something light or the Flying Coffin IPA if you're a hop-head. It's a laid-back spot to refuel and chat about the day's highlights.

## Day 3: Park Exploration & A Sweet Farewell

Your final day is dedicated to one of the crown jewels of the Florida Panhandle: St. Andrews State Park. It's the perfect place to experience the natural beauty that first drew people to this coastline.

### Morning: Discover St. Andrews State Park

Plan to spend at least half a day here—it's that good. **St. Andrews State Park** ([4607 State Park Ln, Panama City, FL 32408](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4607+State+Park+Ln+Panama+City+FL+32408)) offers a little bit of everything.

**Crucial Tip:** Go early! On busy weekends and throughout the summer, the park can reach capacity and close to new visitors by 10 or 11 AM. Arrive before 9 AM to be safe.

Once inside, here’s how to spend your time:

*   **The Jetties:** This is the most popular spot. A rock jetty extends into the pass, creating a calm, protected area that's perfect for snorkeling. The water is often crystal clear, and you can see a surprising amount of fish, crabs, and even octopus.
*   **The "Kiddie Pool":** Just behind the jetty is a large, shallow, protected lagoon. The water is calm and warm, making it the ideal spot for families with young children to splash around safely.
*   **Gulf-Side Beach:** The park also has over a mile of pristine, undeveloped beachfront. It's often less crowded than the main beaches in PCB, with beautiful dunes and soft sand.
*   **Shell Island Shuttle:** The park is the primary departure point for the official shuttle to Shell Island, a 7-mile long undeveloped barrier island. If you want to learn more about this pristine paradise, check out our [complete guide to visiting Shell Island](https://www.springlinestays.com/blog/shell-island-guide).

Pack a cooler with drinks and snacks, as the concessions are limited. You could easily spend the entire day here and not get bored.



### Lunch: A Picnic or a Local Legend

Your best bet for lunch is to pack a picnic to enjoy on the beach at St. Andrews. There are several pavilions available if you need a break from the sun.

If you didn't pack a lunch, as you leave the park, stop at **Scotty's Bottle Shop** ([4720 Thomas Dr](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4720+Thomas+Dr+Panama+City+FL)). It might look like a simple convenience store, but go to the deli in the back. They make some of the best, no-frills steamed sandwiches in the area. It’s an old-school local spot that’s been around forever.

### Afternoon: One Last Souvenir & A Sweet Treat

Spend your last afternoon with some leisurely souvenir shopping. Skip the giant t-shirt chains and check out a local surf shop like **Mr. Surf's** ([7220 Thomas Dr](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=7220+Thomas+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL)) for authentic gear and local art.

Before you head back to pack, treat yourself to one last taste of vacation. **The Pink Pelican Ice Cream Bar** ([7320 Thomas Dr](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=7320+Thomas+Dr+Panama+City+Beach+FL)) is a local legend, serving up delicious ice cream and retro vibes. My personal favorite is the "Trash Can"—it sounds weird, but it's a glorious mix of seven different candies and three syrups in vanilla ice cream. It's the perfect sweet ending to your trip.

## Panama City Beach Itinerary FAQs

**What is the best month to visit Panama City Beach?**
While summer (June-August) is the most popular time, the "shoulder seasons" are arguably the best. September and October offer warm Gulf waters, fewer crowds, and lower humidity. April and May are also fantastic, with beautiful weather before the peak summer season kicks off.

**Is 3 days enough for Panama City Beach?**
Three days is the perfect amount of time for a fantastic long weekend. It allows you to experience the main highlights—the beach, Pier Park, and a state park—without feeling rushed. You'll get a great taste of the area and will likely leave already planning your return trip to explore more.

**What is there to do in Panama City Beach besides the beach?**
Plenty! You can explore the trail systems at the PCB Conservation Park or St. Andrews State Park, go shopping and dining at Pier Park, visit Gulf World Marine Park, take a dolphin-watching tour, or go fishing on a charter boat. The area is rich in both natural beauty and entertainment options away from the sand.

**How do you get around Panama City Beach without a car?**
While having a car is most convenient, you can get by without one if you stay in a central location like the area around Pier Park. This area is very walkable. For longer distances, ride-sharing services like Uber and Lyft are readily available. There is also the Bay Town Trolley, which runs along the main routes, but its schedule can be limited.
