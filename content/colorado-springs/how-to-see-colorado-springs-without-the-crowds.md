## Garden of the Gods: The Sunrise & Sunset Strategy

Garden of the Gods is a National Natural Landmark and it's completely free, which means from about 9 AM to 4 PM, especially on a sunny summer day, it can feel more like a theme park than a nature preserve. The main parking lot fills up, traffic crawls along the one-way scenic loop, and the Central Garden Trails are shoulder-to-shoulder. Here's how to get the park almost all to yourself.

### The Early Bird Gets the View
Sunrise is, without a doubt, the most magical time in the park. The gates open at 5 AM, and you should aim to be there right then. Drive the main loop and park at the Main Parking Lot (North). From there, it's a short, paved walk into the Central Garden Trails. The light that spills over Pikes Peak and illuminates the red rock fins is breathtaking. You’ll share the trails with a few dedicated photographers and maybe some local trail runners, but that's it. You can hear the birds, feel the quiet of the morning, and truly appreciate the scale of the formations.

**Local Tip:** For the absolute best sunrise photo, head towards the Siamese Twins Trail. The short loop hike features a natural window in the rock that perfectly frames Pikes Peak. In the early morning, it's a truly unforgettable shot.

### The Sunset & Evening Wind-Down
If you’re not a morning person, the late evening is your next best bet. Most visitors clear out by 5 PM to head to dinner. From 6 PM until the park closes (9 PM in winter, 10 PM in summer), the crowds thin dramatically. An evening stroll on the paved paths is peaceful and cool, and the setting sun paints the rocks in deep oranges and purples. It’s also the best time to see wildlife like mule deer, who come out to graze in the quieter hours.

![peaceful sunset over Garden of the Gods red rocks](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/peaceful-sunset-over-garden-of-the-gods-red-rocks-fe084e.jpg)

### Ditch the Main Loop for Secluded Hikes
The majority of visitors stick to the paved, 1.5-mile Perkins Central Garden Trail. To truly escape, explore the park’s less-traveled outer trails.

*   **Chambers/Bretag/Palmer Trail:** This 3-mile loop circles the entire park, offering incredible views of the main rock formations from a distance, with Pikes Peak as a constant backdrop. It's a moderate dirt trail with rolling hills and far fewer people.
*   **Scotsman/Buckskin Charlie Trail:** This trail on the park's west side offers a different perspective and is often nearly empty. You'll get stunning views back towards the Central Garden with the city lights twinkling in the distance as evening approaches.

## Pikes Peak: Why the Cog Railway Is Your Best Friend

Reaching the 14,115-foot summit of "America's Mountain" is a bucket-list item for any Colorado Springs trip. The most common way up is the Pikes Peak Highway, but this can be a surprisingly stressful experience. You need a timed entry reservation in the summer, the traffic can be slow, and the hairpin turns and steep drop-offs aren't for the faint of heart.

### The Stress-Free Summit: Pikes Peak Cog Railway
The Broadmoor Manitou and Pikes Peak Cog Railway is, hands down, the best way to the top. You'll board the train in Manitou Springs and enjoy a 9-mile scenic journey through ponderosa pine forests, past boulder fields, and above the treeline into the alpine tundra.

**Why it's better:**
*   **No Driving Stress:** Sit back, relax, and let the conductor narrate the journey. No white-knuckle driving required.
*   **Guaranteed Summit Access:** Your ticket is your reservation. No worrying about road closures due to weather or capacity.
*   **Unobstructed Views:** The large windows provide panoramic views you simply can't get while focusing on the road.

**Local Tip:** Book your tickets *weeks* or even *months* in advance, especially for summer or fall weekends. Seats sell out fast. Also, try to book a seat on the "3-seat" side of the car on the way up for the most expansive views of the reservoir and landscape. Don't forget to grab one of their world-famous high-altitude donuts at the summit!

### If You Must Drive...
If you're set on driving the Pikes Peak Highway, strategy is key.
1.  **Book Your Timed Entry Permit:** This is mandatory from late May to September. Book it online the moment you know your travel dates.
2.  **Go Early on a Weekday:** The first time slot of the day (usually around 7:30 AM) is your best bet to beat the worst of the traffic. Weekdays are significantly less busy than weekends.
3.  **Check the Weather & Road Status:** The weather at the summit can be wildly different from the city. Call the pre-recorded information line at (719) 385-7325 before you go.

## Manitou Springs & The Incline: Master the Reservation, Find the Alternative

The charming, quirky town of Manitou Springs and the lung-busting Manitou Incline are two of the region's most popular attractions. This also makes them two of the most crowded.

### Conquering the Manitou Incline
This legendary trail gains nearly 2,000 feet of elevation in less than a mile—it's not a hike, it's a challenge. Due to its popularity, a free online reservation system is in place to manage the crowds.

**The Real Secret:** It’s not just about booking in advance. The key to a good experience is booking the *first time slot of the day* (usually around 6:00 AM). Why? Because even with reservations, you can get stuck in a human traffic jam on the ties. By being in the first group, you have a clearer path ahead and you'll be climbing in the cool morning shade instead of the brutal afternoon sun. Remember to take the scenic 4-mile Barr Trail back down; descending the Incline is strongly discouraged and dangerous.

### The Best Incline Alternative: Section 16
If you can't get an Incline reservation or want a similar workout without the rigid structure, head to Section 16 and the Palmer Loop Trail. The trailhead is located off Gold Camp Road ([Bear Creek Cañon Park, 24th St](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bear+Creek+Cañon+Park,+24th+St+Colorado+Springs+CO)). The initial climb is brutally steep, much like the Incline, but it soon levels out into a beautiful 6-mile loop with fantastic views of Garden of the Gods and the city. It’s a local favorite for a reason.

![hiker looking out over Colorado Springs from a mountain trail](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/hiker-looking-out-over-colorado-springs-from-a-mou-d2e63f.jpg)

### Navigating Manitou Springs
Parking in downtown Manitou Springs is notoriously difficult and expensive. On a summer weekend, you can spend 30 minutes just looking for a spot. The solution is simple: **don't drive into downtown**. Park at the free lot located at [10 Old Man's Trail](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=10+Old+Man's+Trail+Manitou+Springs+CO) and take the free shuttle (Route 33) that runs every 20 minutes and loops through town and up to the Cog Railway station. It’s easy, stress-free, and saves you a headache.

## Ditching the Crowds Entirely: Three Peaceful Alternatives

Sometimes you just want to enjoy the Pikes Peak region's beauty without a plan or a reservation. When the main attractions feel too busy, pivot to these incredible, less-trafficked spots.

### 1. Red Rock Canyon Open Space
Think of Red Rock Canyon as Garden of the Gods' more laid-back sibling. Located just south of Highway 24, this 1,474-acre park features the same stunning red rock formations, but with a fraction of the crowds. It's a maze of easy-to-moderate trails popular with local hikers, trail runners, and rock climbers. The Contemplative Trail and the Red Rock Rim Trail offer fantastic views and a sense of solitude that can be hard to find elsewhere. There's even a fun, free-ride bike park and two off-leash dog loops.

### 2. North Cheyenne Cañon Park
While tourists flock to the (paid) Seven Falls, locals head to the (free) North Cheyenne Cañon Park. This city-owned gem is a 1,600-foot-deep canyon with a scenic drive that winds alongside the creek.

*   **Helen Hunt Falls:** An easily accessible and beautiful waterfall right off the main road. A short, steep hike from the visitor center takes you to the top of the falls for an even better view.
*   **Starsmore Visitor and Nature Center:** A great starting point at the park's entrance to grab a map and learn about the area's geology and wildlife.
*   **Columbine Trail:** This trail follows the creek up the canyon, offering a shaded, peaceful hike away from the road. It’s perfect for a hot summer day.

### 3. Old Colorado City (on a Weekday Morning)
Old Colorado City is a must-visit, but its narrow sidewalks can get packed on weekend afternoons. To really enjoy its historic charm, art galleries, and unique boutiques, visit on a Tuesday or Wednesday morning. Grab a coffee and a pastry from Carnelian Coffee Co. ([2302 W Colorado Ave](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2302+W+Colorado+Ave+Colorado+Springs+CO)), and wander the streets before the lunch rush. You can pop into art galleries and have a real conversation with the owners or browse the shops without feeling rushed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the least crowded time to visit Colorado Springs?
The shoulder seasons—specifically September/October and April/May—are the best times to visit for fewer crowds and pleasant weather. If you visit in the summer, aim for mid-week travel (Tuesday-Thursday) as weekends are significantly busier.

### Do I need a reservation for Garden of the Gods?
No, Garden of the Gods Park is free and does not require a reservation to enter. However, arriving very early (before 8 AM) or late (after 5 PM) is highly recommended to find parking and avoid the biggest crowds, especially in the summer.

### What's a good alternative to the Manitou Incline?
Section 16 Trail in Bear Creek Cañon Park is a fantastic local-favorite alternative. It offers a similarly strenuous uphill challenge at the beginning but without the need for a reservation. The full Palmer Loop is about 6 miles and provides incredible views of the city and surrounding landscape.

### Is it better to drive or take the Cog Railway up Pikes Peak?
For most visitors, the Cog Railway is the superior experience. It eliminates the stress of mountain driving, navigating traffic, and securing a timed entry permit for the highway. You get to relax and enjoy panoramic views the entire way up. The drive offers more flexibility to stop at viewpoints, but the train is a more relaxing and guaranteed way to reach the summit.

### Where is the best area to stay in Colorado Springs to avoid traffic?
Staying on the west side of town, near Old Colorado City or just west of I-25, provides the best access to the majority of attractions (Garden of the Gods, Pikes Peak, Manitou Springs, Red Rock Canyon) while avoiding the city's main commuter traffic. This positioning allows you to get a head start on your day and beat the rush.
