## Your 4-Day Colorado Springs Group Itinerary

This itinerary is designed for maximum fun and minimum stress. It balances iconic sights with flexible options, ensuring everyone from your energetic nephew to your history-loving grandmother has a fantastic time.

### Day 1: Garden of the Gods & Old Colorado City Charm

**Morning (8:30 AM - 12:00 PM): Conquer the Garden of the Gods**

You can't come to Colorado Springs and not see Garden of the Gods. The key with a large group is to **go early**. Aim to arrive before 9 AM to secure parking spots near each other at the main lot off of Juniper Way Loop.

*   **The Plan:** Walk the **Perkins Central Garden Trail**. It's a 1.5-mile paved loop that is wheelchair and stroller accessible, making it perfect for all ages and mobility levels. It winds right through the base of the most famous red rock formations. You'll get all the epic photos without any strenuous climbing.
*   **Local Tip:** Before you enter the main park, stop at the Garden of the Gods Visitor & Nature Center at [1805 N 30th St](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1805+N+30th+St+Colorado+Springs+CO). The view of the park with Pikes Peak in the background from the terrace is arguably the best in the city. It’s also the best place for a group bathroom break.

![sweeping view of Garden of the Gods red rocks with Pikes Peak in the background](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/sweeping-view-of-garden-of-the-gods-red-rocks-with-de0734.jpg)

**Lunch (12:30 PM): Group-Friendly Grub**

You have two great options. You can pack a massive picnic and grab a spot at the High Point Overlook picnic area, which offers fantastic views. Or, you can head straight to Old Colorado City, just a 5-minute drive away. For a group, **[Front Range Barbeque](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2330+W+Colorado+Ave+Colorado+Springs+CO)** is a crowd-pleaser with a great patio and food that satisfies everyone.

**Afternoon (2:00 PM - 5:00 PM): Wander Old Colorado City**

This is the perfect "split up and meet back later" activity. Let people explore the historic district's art galleries, boutique shops, and candy stores at their own pace. Set a meeting point and time, like in front of the whimsical **[Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2431+W+Colorado+Ave+Colorado+Springs+CO)**.

**Evening (6:00 PM Onward): Dinner & Downtime**

After a full day, retreat to your rental. This is the beauty of having a home base. Fire up the grill for a low-key BBQ, or make a giant pasta dinner in the kitchen. It’s more relaxing and budget-friendly than coordinating a restaurant reservation for 12. ### Day 2: Reach New Heights on Pikes Peak

**Morning (9:00 AM - 1:00 PM): America's Mountain**

Today is all about Pikes Peak. You have two primary ways to reach the 14,115-foot summit.

*   **Option 1: The Broadmoor Manitou and Pikes Peak Cog Railway.** For most large groups, this is the superior choice. No one has to navigate winding mountain roads, you can all sit together, and the views are spectacular. **You MUST book tickets weeks, if not months, in advance**, especially for a large party. The depot is at [515 Ruxton Ave, Manitou Springs](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=515+Ruxton+Ave+Manitou+Springs+CO).
*   **Option 2: Drive the Pikes Peak Highway.** This offers more flexibility but is logistically harder with multiple cars. From May through September, you'll need to make a timed entry reservation online. The drive is beautiful but can be nerve-wracking for those not used to mountain driving.

**Local Tip:** Regardless of how you get up, bring layers! It can be 30-40 degrees colder at the summit than in the city. Also, remind everyone to drink lots of water to help with the altitude.

**Lunch (1:00 PM): World-Famous Donuts**

Once at the top, head into the Summit Visitor Center. The one and only thing you must eat here are the high-altitude donuts. They have a unique recipe that only works above 14,000 feet. Grab a dozen (or three) for the group. It's the perfect reward.

**Afternoon (2:30 PM Onward): High-Altitude Recovery**

Everyone will be feeling the effects of the altitude and the early start. This afternoon is all about rest and relaxation back at your vacation rental. This is where amenities like a hot tub or a comfortable living room with stunning mountain views become the main event. Let people nap, read, or just stare at the scenery.

**Dinner (6:30 PM): Casual Downtown Dinner**

Head downtown for a casual dinner. **[Phantom Canyon Brewing Company](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2+E+Pikes+Peak+Ave+Colorado+Springs+CO)** is a great spot for groups. It's a historic, multi-level building with a huge menu that goes way beyond typical pub fare, so even the non-beer-drinkers will find something they love.

### Day 3: Choose Your Own Adventure

By day three, you'll have a sense of your group's energy levels and interests. The best way to keep everyone happy is to offer a few choices. Divide and conquer!

![family feeding giraffes at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo with mountains in the background](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/family-feeding-giraffes-at-cheyenne-mountain-zoo-w-8f33bb.jpg)

#### Option A: For the Animal Lovers
Spend the day at the **[Cheyenne Mountain Zoo](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4250+Cheyenne+Mountain+Zoo+Rd+Colorado+Springs+CO)**. It's America's only mountain zoo, built right into the side of a mountain. The enclosures are naturalistic, and the views of the city are incredible. The absolute must-do activity is feeding the giraffe herd—they have one of the largest in the country. It’s an unforgettable experience for all ages.

#### Option B: For the Hikers and Scenery Seekers
Explore **Red Rock Canyon Open Space**. It's like a smaller, free version of Garden of the Gods. It has a network of easy-to-moderate trails, making it easy for people to choose a walk that suits their fitness level. The former quarry area is a fun, unique landscape to explore.

#### Option C: For the Thrill-Seekers and History Buffs
Head to **[Seven Falls](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1+Lodge+Rd+Colorado+Springs+CO)**. This series of seven cascading waterfalls in a box canyon is a dramatic sight. You can climb the 224 steps alongside the falls for a workout and a great view, or take the in-mountain elevator for an easier experience. It's a privately owned attraction, so expect a per-person entrance fee.

**Evening: Game Night Extravaganza**

Tonight, it's all about enjoying your rental. Order a stack of pizzas and make use of your home's amenities. This is where a property like the **[Stunning Mountain Views, Hot Tub, Game Room](https://book.springlinestays.com/property/family-friendly-firepit-hottub-views-games)** shines. While the kids (and adults!) battle it out in the game room, others can be soaking in the hot tub under the stars.

### Day 4: Quirky Manitou Springs & The Road Home

**Morning (9:30 AM - 12:30 PM): Explore Manitou Springs**

Before you pack up and head out, spend your last morning in the quirky, historic town of Manitou Springs.

*   **The Plan:** Stroll through the downtown area, popping into the unique shops and art galleries. The real fun is finding and tasting the water from the eight naturally carbonated mineral springs scattered around town. The waters have different mineral contents and tastes. Bring some small cups for everyone to try.
*   **Don't Miss:** The **Manitou Penny Arcade** is a must-visit. It's a massive, rambling collection of vintage and modern arcade games, from classic pinball to old-school skeeball. Give everyone a roll of quarters and let them loose.

![a colorful, historic street in downtown Manitou Springs, Colorado](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/a-colorful-historic-street-in-downtown-manitou-spr-0c44e5.jpg)

**Lunch (12:30 PM): A Final Meal Together**

Have one last group meal at a Manitou institution. **[Adam's Mountain Cafe](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=934+Manitou+Ave+Manitou+Springs+CO)** is a fantastic choice, known for its delicious, globally-inspired cuisine with plenty of vegetarian and vegan options to satisfy everyone's dietary needs.

After lunch, it's time to head back to your rental for final packing and a smooth departure, full of memories from a perfectly executed group trip.

## Pro-Tips for Large Group Travel in Colorado Springs

*   **Book Everything in Advance:** This is the golden rule. Your vacation rental, the Cog Railway, and any special dinner reservations should be booked weeks or even months ahead.
*   **Designate a Leader:** Have one person be the point person for reservations and tickets to avoid double bookings or confusion.
*   **Embrace Downtime:** Don't overschedule your days. The best moments often happen during unplanned time, just hanging out at the house.
*   **Use the Kitchen:** Eating out for every meal with a group of 10+ is expensive and time-consuming. Plan to cook simple breakfasts and at least a couple of dinners at your rental. It saves money and is often more relaxing.

## Colorado Springs Group Trip FAQ

**What is the best time of year for a large group to visit Colorado Springs?**
Summer (June-August) offers the best weather for all outdoor activities, but it's also the busiest. For fewer crowds and beautiful fall colors, September is a fantastic choice. The weather is still pleasant, and major attractions are less crowded.

**Is Colorado Springs walkable for a large group?**
The individual historic districts like Old Colorado City, Manitou Springs, and parts of Downtown are very walkable. However, you will absolutely need cars (or to coordinate a large van rental) to travel between these areas and get to attractions like Garden of the Gods, Pikes Peak, and the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo.

**How many days do you really need for a group trip to Colorado Springs?**
Four full days, as outlined in this itinerary, is the sweet spot. It allows you to see the main highlights without feeling rushed and provides enough flexibility and downtime to keep a large group happy and rested.

**Where can a large group eat in Colorado Springs without a reservation?**
For casual, no-reservation-needed spots that are great for groups, consider food halls like **[CO.A.T.I U.S.](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=514+S+Tejon+St+Colorado+Springs+CO)** or **[The Well](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=315+E+Pikes+Peak+Ave+%23100+Colorado+Springs+CO)**. Everyone can order from different food stalls to suit their own tastes.
