## Day 1: The Red Rocks & Old West Charm

Your first day is all about diving headfirst into the iconic landscapes that define Colorado Springs. We're hitting Garden of the Gods, but we're going to do it like locals.

### Morning: Garden of the Gods National Natural Landmark

**The Plan:** Get here early. I'm talking 7:30 AM early, especially if you're here between May and September. The light is softer, the crowds are non-existent, and you might actually find a parking spot at the main lot for the Perkins Central Garden Trail. This paved, 1.5-mile loop is non-negotiable. It winds you through the base of the most famous formations like Kissing Camels and Sentinel Spires.

![sunrise casting long shadows on Garden of the Gods red rock formations](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/sunrise-casting-long-shadows-on-garden-of-the-gods-262b2b.jpg)

**Local Tip:** Most people just do the main loop. Don't be most people. Grab a trail map and hike the Chambers/Bretag/Palmer Trail. It's a slightly more rugged 3-mile loop that offers incredible perspectives of the park with Pikes Peak perfectly framed in the background. You’ll leave 90% of the crowds behind.

*   **Address:** [1805 N 30th St, Colorado Springs, CO 80904](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1805+N+30th+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80904)
*   **Cost:** Absolutely free!

### Afternoon: Lunch and Exploring Old Colorado City

After a morning of hiking, you'll be just a few minutes' drive from Old Colorado City (OCC), the original capital of the Colorado Territory. It’s packed with historic charm, art galleries, and fantastic places to eat.

**Lunch Spot:** Head straight to [Pizzeria Rustica](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2527+W+Colorado+Ave,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80904). Their wood-fired Neapolitan pizzas are the real deal. The *Funghi* pizza with a glass of their house red is the perfect post-hike refuel.

After lunch, spend an hour or two wandering down Colorado Avenue. Pop into the art galleries and unique shops. Don't miss Michael Garman's Magic Town, a quirky and incredibly detailed miniature city that's part history, part sculpture.

### Evening: A Relaxed Dinner & Fireside Chat

For dinner, keep it casual at [Front Range Barbeque](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2330+W+Colorado+Ave,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80904). It’s a local institution with killer smoked meats and a great patio. Then, head back to your home base to relax.

## Day 2: Conquering America's Mountain

Today is Pikes Peak day. At 14,115 feet, this is the mountain that inspired the song "America the Beautiful," and the views from the summit are genuinely breathtaking (in more ways than one).

### Getting to the Top: Drive vs. Cog Railway

You have two main options for ascending the peak:

1.  **Drive the Pikes Peak Highway:** This 19-mile paved road is a stunning, white-knuckle drive with switchbacks and sheer drop-offs. It gives you the freedom to stop at viewpoints along the way. **Important:** From late May to September, you *must* [make a reservation online](https://www.pikespeakcolorado.com/) for a timed entry slot to drive to the summit. Don't just show up.
2.  **The Broadmoor Manitou and Pikes Peak Cog Railway:** This is the stress-free option. The 3.5-hour round trip in a comfortable train car lets you focus entirely on the scenery, from lush forests to the alpine tundra. Book your tickets well in advance, as they sell out.

**Local Tip:** Whichever you choose, go in the morning. Colorado’s classic afternoon thunderstorms can roll in quickly at high altitudes.

### At the Summit

The new Summit Visitor Center is incredible. Grab one of their world-famous high-altitude donuts (they have a special recipe that works up there) and walk around the observation decks. The air is thin, so take it easy. Drink lots of water today, starting the day before.

*   **Pikes Peak Highway Entrance:** [4750 Pikes Peak Highway, Cascade, CO 80809](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4750+Pikes+Peak+Highway,+Cascade,+CO+80809)
*   **Cog Railway Depot:** [515 Ruxton Ave, Manitou Springs, CO 80829](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=515+Ruxton+Ave,+Manitou+Springs,+CO+80829)

### Evening: A Well-Deserved Meal

You'll be tired and hungry after your high-altitude adventure. Treat yourself to dinner at [The Rabbit Hole](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=101+N+Tejon+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903), a unique underground restaurant downtown with creative New American cuisine and fantastic cocktails. The entrance is a subtle subway staircase—finding it is part of the fun.

## Day 3: Quirky Manitou & Canyon Hikes

After conquering a fourteener, today is about exploring the funky, free-spirited town at its base: Manitou Springs.

### Morning: Explore Manitou Springs

Start your day by walking through the historic downtown of Manitou Springs. The main drag is lined with art galleries, bohemian boutiques, and psychics. But the real draw is the town's namesake: the eight naturally carbonated mineral springs. Grab a map and a tasting cup from the Visitor Center and go on a self-guided tour to taste the unique mineral water from each font.

Afterward, unleash your inner child at the Manitou Springs Penny Arcade. It’s a massive collection of vintage and modern arcade games, with many still costing just a penny to play.

### Afternoon: Red Rock Canyon Open Space

For your afternoon hike, skip the crowds that might be at Garden of the Gods and head to [Red Rock Canyon Open Space](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Red+Rock+Canyon+Open+Space,+Colorado+Springs,+CO) instead. It features the same stunning red rock fins but is much larger and less congested. The Red Rock Canyon/Hogback Valley Trail is a great 2.5-mile loop that gives you a fantastic tour of the park's geology.

![hiker looking at a map in Red Rock Canyon Open Space Colorado Springs](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/hiker-looking-at-a-map-in-red-rock-canyon-open-spa-ab63a2.jpg)

**For the Adrenaline Junkies:** If you’re feeling *extremely* ambitious, today is your day to tackle the Manitou Incline. This is not a hike; it's a challenge. You’ll gain 2,000 feet of elevation in less than a mile up the ties of an old funicular railway. It's brutal, but the bragging rights are forever. Reservations are required.

### Evening: Dinner with a View

Head to [The Keg Lounge](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=730+Manitou+Ave,+Manitou+Springs,+CO+80829) in Manitou for great burgers and a laid-back local atmosphere. It's the perfect place to unwind after a day of exploring.

## Day 4: High-Altitude Wildlife & Waterfalls

Today we head south to explore the Cheyenne Cañon area, home to two of the city's most beloved attractions.

### Morning: Cheyenne Mountain Zoo

This is not your average zoo. Built into the side of a mountain, the [Cheyenne Mountain Zoo](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4250+Cheyenne+Mountain+Zoo+Rd,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80906) is America’s only mountain zoo, offering incredible views of the city below.

**Must-Do:** The giraffe feeding experience. You get to stand on a platform at eye-level with the world's largest giraffe herd and feed them lettuce right from your hand. It's an unforgettable experience for all ages. Book your tickets and your giraffe feeding time slot online in advance. Wear comfortable shoes; the hills are no joke.

### Afternoon: The Seven Falls

Just down the road from the zoo is [The Broadmoor Seven Falls](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1+Lake+Ave,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80906). This series of seven cascading waterfalls is situated in a stunning 1,250-foot-wall box canyon. You have to park at the Broadmoor Hotel and take a dedicated shuttle to the entrance.

Once there, you can climb the 224 steps alongside the falls to reach hiking trails at the top, or take the in-mountain elevator for a fantastic view from the "Eagle's Nest" platform.

**Local Tip:** The ticket for Seven Falls is a bit pricey. If you want a similar (and free) canyon waterfall experience, head to the nearby North Cheyenne Cañon Park and hike the short trail to Helen Hunt Falls instead.

## Day 5: A Day Trip to the Royal Gorge

Ready to see one of Colorado's most mind-boggling man-made wonders? Today we're taking a day trip about an hour southwest to Cañon City.

### The Main Event: Royal Gorge Bridge & Park

The [Royal Gorge Bridge](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4218+Co+Rd+3A,+Cañon+City,+CO+81212) is America's highest suspension bridge, hanging 956 feet above the roaring Arkansas River. Walking across it is a thrill, but the park offers so much more.

*   **Aerial Gondola:** A silent, breathtaking ride across the gorge. Do this first for the best photos of the bridge.
*   **Cloudscraper Zip Line:** For the truly brave, this sends you soaring 1,200 feet above the river.
*   **Royal Rush Skycoaster:** Essentially a giant swing that launches you out over the edge of the canyon.

Plan to spend at least 3-4 hours here. The ticket includes the bridge, gondola, and a few other attractions. The thrill rides are extra.

![Royal Gorge Bridge spanning the canyon with the Arkansas River below](/static/images/blog/colorado-springs/royal-gorge-bridge-spanning-the-canyon-with-the-ar-438abf.jpg)

### Lunch in Cañon City

After the park, head into the small town of Cañon City for lunch. [The Owl Cigar Store](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=626+Main+St,+Cañon+City,+CO+81212) (it's a diner, not a cigar shop) is a classic spot for burgers and milkshakes.

### Alternative: Whitewater Rafting

If heights aren't your thing, the Arkansas River that carved the Royal Gorge offers some of the best whitewater rafting in the state. Numerous outfitters in Cañon City offer half-day and full-day trips through Bighorn Sheep Canyon (family-friendly) or the Royal Gorge itself (more advanced).

## Day 6: Olympic Dreams & Downtown Vibes

After a big day trip, we'll stay local today and explore the legacy of "Olympic City USA."

### Morning: U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Museum

This is one of the newest and most impressive attractions in Colorado Springs. The [U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=200+S+Sierra+Madre+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903) is a stunning piece of architecture filled with state-of-the-art interactive exhibits. Even if you're not a huge sports fan, the stories of the athletes are incredibly inspiring. You can race against a virtual Jesse Owens, try your hand at alpine skiing, and see a massive collection of Olympic torches. Plan for 2-3 hours.

### Afternoon: Explore Downtown Colorado Springs

The museum is conveniently located on the edge of downtown. Spend the afternoon exploring the area. Tejon Street is the main north-south artery, lined with local shops, restaurants, and bars.

*   **Shopping:** Stop by [Poor Richard's](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=320+N+Tejon+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903), a beloved local complex that includes a bookstore, toy store, restaurant, and wine bar all in one.
*   **Coffee Break:** Grab an expertly crafted latte at [Story Coffee Company](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=120+E+Bijou+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903), a tiny home that was converted into one of the city's best coffee shops.

### Evening: A Taste of the New West

Downtown Colorado Springs has a fantastic and growing food scene. For dinner, make a reservation at [Four by Brother Luck](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=321+N+Tejon+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903). Chef Brother Luck (of *Top Chef* fame) creates dishes inspired by the Four Corners region, and the food is both inventive and delicious. Afterward, catch a craft cocktail at the speakeasy-style [Shame & Regret](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=15+E+Bijou+St+B,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903).

## Day 7: Brunch, Art & A Final View

It's your last day. Let's make it a relaxing and memorable one before you head home.

### Morning: The Ultimate Brunch

You can't leave town without having a great brunch. Head to [Urban Egg](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=28+S+Tejon+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903) for an amazing selection of pancakes, benedicts, and morning cocktails. The Rocky Mountain Harvest pancake is a local favorite. Get there early or join the waitlist online; it's always busy for a reason.

### Afternoon: Culture or Scenery

Choose your final adventure:

*   **For the Art Lover:** Visit the [Colorado Springs Fine Arts Center at Colorado College](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=30+W+Dale+St,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80903). It has a fantastic collection of American, Native American, and Spanish Colonial art, all housed in a beautiful Art Deco building.
*   **For One Last View:** Drive up to [Palmer Park](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Palmer+Park,+Colorado+Springs,+CO). This city park is a geological oddity, with rock formations and scenic overlooks that provide a panoramic view of the entire city with Pikes Peak as the backdrop. It's the perfect spot to reflect on your incredible week.

### Evening: Final Sunset

Grab a casual pizza and a beer at [Colorado Mountain Brewery](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=600+S+21st+St+100,+Colorado+Springs,+CO+80904) at the Roundhouse, and watch the setting sun paint the mountains in alpenglow. It's the perfect farewell.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best month to visit Colorado Springs?
For pleasant weather for hiking and sightseeing, June through September are ideal. The fall months (September and October) offer beautiful autumn colors and fewer crowds. If you're interested in winter sports, January and February are best, but be prepared for snow and colder temperatures.

### Do I need a car in Colorado Springs?
Yes, a car is highly recommended. While you can explore specific neighborhoods like Downtown or Old Colorado City on foot, the main attractions like Pikes Peak, Garden of the Gods, and Cheyenne Cañon are spread out. A car will give you the flexibility to follow this itinerary and discover hidden gems.

### How many days are enough for Colorado Springs?
While you can see the main highlights in a long weekend (3-4 days), a full week allows you to explore the area at a more relaxed pace, take a day trip like the one to the Royal Gorge, and discover some of the local favorites beyond the big-ticket attractions. This 7-day Colorado Springs travel guide shows just how much there is to do!

### Is Colorado Springs expensive to visit?
Colorado Springs can be a very affordable destination. Many of its best attractions, like Garden of the Gods, Red Rock Canyon Open Space, and North Cheyenne Cañon Park, are completely free.
