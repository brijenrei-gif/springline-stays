Colorado Springs Neighborhood Guide: Where to Stay for the Perfect Trip

Finding the perfect place to stay in Colorado Springs isn’t just about the number of beds. It’s about the vibe. Are you looking for a walkable, historic neighborhood filled with art galleries, or a quiet, scenic basecamp with trailheads right outside your door? The neighborhood you choose is the backdrop for your entire adventure, shaping everything from your morning coffee run to your evening wind-down.

Instead of just dropping a pin on a map, let’s break down the city’s distinct areas. Think of this as your inside guide to finding the perfect home base, ensuring your Colorado Springs vacation rental feels less like a hotel and more like your personal launchpad to the Rockies.

The Westside: Historic Charm at the Foot of the Mountains

The Vibe: Artsy, independent, and undeniably historic. The Westside, encompassing Old Colorado City and the gateway to Manitou Springs, feels like a town all its own. Victorian architecture houses quirky boutiques, fantastic art galleries, and some of the best independent restaurants in the city. The mountains aren’t just a view here; they’re your backyard. You’re minutes from the sandstone fins of Garden of the Gods and the challenging ascent of the Manitou Incline.

Perfect For: History buffs, art lovers, hikers, and anyone who prefers strolling to a local coffee shop over driving to a chain. If your ideal day involves a morning hike, an afternoon of gallery-hopping, and an evening at a cozy, one-of-a-kind restaurant, this is your spot.

What to Do on the Westside

  • Explore Old Colorado City: Don’t just stick to Colorado Avenue. Wander down the side streets to find hidden courtyards and unique shops. The First Friday Art Walk (held the first Friday of every month) is a must-do, with galleries staying open late, offering snacks, wine, and a chance to meet the artists.
  • Hike Red Rock Canyon Open Space: Often called the “local’s Garden of the Gods,” this park at 3550 W High St offers stunning red rock formations and miles of trails with a fraction of the crowds. The Contemplative Trail is a great, easy loop for families.
  • Day Trip to Manitou Springs: This eccentric mountain town is just a 10-minute drive away. Play vintage games at the Penny Arcade, drink the “healing” mineral water from the public fountains, and browse the eclectic shops.
  • Conquer the Manitou Incline: For the truly ambitious, this legendary trail gains nearly 2,000 feet of elevation in less than a mile. It’s a serious workout, but the views are your reward. Pro tip: You must make a free reservation online in advance, as walk-ups are not allowed.

Where to Eat & Drink

  • Morning Fuel: Carnelian Coffee Co. on 2221 W Colorado Ave is the go-to for expertly crafted lattes and a cozy atmosphere to plan your day.
  • Lunch/Dinner: Paravicini’s Italian Bistro (2802 W Colorado Ave) is a Westside institution for a reason. It’s loud, it’s fun, and the Sunday Gravy is phenomenal. For something more casual, Tapateria’s Spanish-inspired small plates are perfect for sharing.
  • Evening Drinks: Mother Muff’s is a classic dive bar, but for something a bit more refined, the wines and charcuterie boards at Sweet Elephant Vino are fantastic.

Where to Stay on the Westside

For a group or family that wants to be right in the middle of the action, a home base near Old Colorado City is ideal. You get the neighborhood feel with unparalleled access to the main attractions. A place like Views | 2 mins to OCC | Fire Pit | Games | Pets is a perfect example. Located at 1112 Arch St, it’s literally a two-minute drive from the heart of OCC. After a long day of exploring, you can gather around the fire pit, enjoy the mountain views, and recharge for the next day’s adventure.

The Broadmoor & Cheyenne Cañon: Upscale Scenery and Outdoor Adventure

The Vibe: Elegant, established, and surrounded by breathtaking natural beauty. This area on the southwest side of the city is defined by two landmarks: the historic, five-star Broadmoor Hotel and the stunning North Cheyenne Cañon Park. The streets are wide and leafy, the homes are stately, and you’re just as likely to see a deer in your yard as you are a neighbor. It’s quieter than the Westside but offers some of the most dramatic scenery and direct trail access in the city.

Perfect For: Families looking for more space, luxury travelers, and nature lovers who want world-class hiking and attractions like the zoo and Seven Falls right on their doorstep.

What to Do in the Broadmoor Area

  • Visit The Broadmoor: You don’t have to be a guest to experience this iconic hotel. Stroll around Cheyenne Lake, grab a cocktail at the impressive Hotel Bar, or book a lane at Play, their upscale bowling alley. It’s a fantastic way to spend a sophisticated afternoon.
  • Feed the Giraffes at Cheyenne Mountain Zoo: This is consistently ranked one of the best zoos in the country, and for good reason. It’s built on the side of a mountain, offering incredible views of the city. The absolute can’t-miss experience is hand-feeding the massive giraffe herd. Get there early to beat the crowds for this popular attraction.
  • Hike North Cheyenne Cañon Park: This city park is a local treasure. Drive up the scenic road (or hike it!) to Helen Hunt Falls. For a more challenging hike with a massive payoff, take the Columbine Trail from the Starsmore Visitor Center up to the falls and beyond. It’s a gorgeous, shaded trail that follows the creek.
  • Marvel at Seven Falls: Located in a box canyon, this series of seven cascading waterfalls is a true spectacle. You can climb the 224 steps to the top for incredible views or take the in-mountain elevator for an easier ascent.

Where to Eat & Drink

  • Upscale Dining: For a special occasion, La Taverne at The Broadmoor offers legendary steaks in a classic, old-world setting.
  • Casual & Local: Sacred Ground Cafe (1801 W Cheyenne Rd) is a beloved spot for coffee, breakfast burritos, and sandwiches before or after a hike in the cañon.
  • Family-Friendly: Edelweiss German Restaurant (34 E Ramona Ave) is a Colorado Springs classic with a beautiful outdoor beer garden and authentic German food that kids and adults love.

Where to Stay in the Broadmoor Area

This neighborhood is all about finding a comfortable, amenity-rich home to return to after a day of adventure. It’s the perfect area for a larger group or family that values features like a hot tub, game room, and plenty of space to spread out. The Epic Family Home: Mini-Golf, Sauna, Hot Tub, Games at 19 Clover Cir E is exactly what makes this area special. Imagine hiking all morning in Cheyenne Cañon, then coming home to a private sauna, a round of mini-golf in the backyard, and a soak in the hot tub under the stars. It’s a vacation within a vacation.

Downtown: The Urban Heartbeat of the City

The Vibe: Energetic, modern, and central. Downtown Colorado Springs has undergone a massive revitalization and is now a hub of fantastic restaurants, craft cocktail bars, unique shops, and cultural attractions. It’s a true urban center with a walkable grid, public art, and the stunning backdrop of Pikes Peak visible from almost every street corner.

Perfect For: Foodies, couples, museum-goers, and anyone who wants to be in the center of the action with plenty of nightlife and dining options within walking distance.

What to Do Downtown

  • U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Museum: This state-of-the-art, interactive museum is an absolute must-see. It’s an inspiring and brilliantly designed tribute to Team USA athletes. Plan to spend at least 2-3 hours here.
  • Explore Tejon Street: This is the main north-south artery of downtown, lined with the best boutiques, restaurants, and bars. Pop into Poor Richard’s, a beloved local institution that’s a bookstore, toy store, cafe, and pizzeria all in one.
  • Catch a Show: See what’s playing at the Pikes Peak Center for the Performing Arts, home to the Colorado Springs Philharmonic, or check the schedule for a concert at the new Weidner Field, home of the Switchbacks FC soccer team.
  • America the Beautiful Park: Walk across the stunning Park Union Bridge to this large urban park, which features a fantastic playground, fountains, and hosts events and festivals throughout the summer.

Where to Eat & Drink

  • A Foodie Destination: Shuga’s (702 S Cascade Ave) is famous for its quirky decor, global-inspired menu, and the legendary “Shuga’s Grog.” Their spicy shrimp soup is the perfect antidote to a chilly evening.
  • Craft Cocktails: The new speakeasy-style bar, The Archives, hidden beneath a pizza shop, serves up some of the most creative and delicious cocktails in the city. For a rooftop vibe with incredible mountain views, Lumen8 is the spot.
  • Elevated Dinner: The new restaurant Ephemera in the new Kinship Landing hotel is getting rave reviews for its innovative, chef-driven tasting menus.

Northgate & Briargate: Modern Comfort and Air Force Academy Access

The Vibe: Modern, spacious, and convenient. The northern part of Colorado Springs is characterized by newer developments, wide-open spaces, and fantastic Pikes Peak views across the plains. It’s a suburban hub that offers easy access to I-25, the Air Force Academy, and some of the city’s best shopping.

Perfect For: Families visiting the Air Force Academy, groups who want newer homes with modern amenities, and shoppers who appreciate having everything they need right at their fingertips.

What to Do in Northgate & Briargate

  • Visit the United States Air Force Academy: This is one of Colorado’s top tourist attractions. Drive onto the beautiful campus to visit the Barry Goldwater Visitor Center and see the incredible Cadet Chapel (note: the Chapel is currently undergoing extensive restoration and is covered, but the campus is still worth a visit).
  • Shop ‘til You Drop: The Promenade Shops at Briargate is a beautiful outdoor mall with a great mix of national retailers and local boutiques.
  • Hike at Fox Run Regional Park: This large park offers a lovely network of trails through ponderosa pine forests, a beautiful lake, and several playgrounds, making it a perfect spot for a family outing.
  • Get Your Adrenaline Fix: Overdrive Raceway offers high-speed indoor go-karting, and the Great Wolf Lodge has an indoor waterpark that offers day passes for non-guests.

Where to Eat & Drink

  • For the Whole Family: The wide range of restaurants at the Shops at Briargate, like Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar or Modern Market Eatery, makes it easy to please everyone.
  • A Local Favorite: Vaqueros Mexican Restaurant (1805 Briargate Pkwy) is always bustling. They have great margaritas and a massive menu of classic Mexican-American dishes.
  • Date Night: Saltgrass Steak House offers a reliable and delicious option for a classic steakhouse dinner without the downtown price tag.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best area to stay in Colorado Springs for families?

The Broadmoor and Cheyenne Cañon area is fantastic for families. It’s close to top attractions like the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo and Seven Falls, offers spacious homes, and has plenty of parks and open spaces. The Northgate/Briargate area is another great choice for its modern amenities and family-friendly restaurants.

Is it better to stay in Downtown Colorado Springs or Old Colorado City?

It depends on your preference. Stay in Old Colorado City (The Westside) for a historic, artsy, and laid-back vibe with immediate access to Garden of the Gods and Red Rock Canyon. Choose Downtown if you want a more urban, energetic experience with a wider variety of modern restaurants, bars, and cultural attractions like the Olympic Museum.

Do I need a car in Colorado Springs?

Yes, it is highly recommended. While some neighborhoods like Downtown and Old Colorado City are very walkable within their boundaries, Colorado Springs is a sprawling city. To access the best hiking trails and attractions like Pikes Peak, the Air Force Academy, and the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo, you will absolutely need a car.

Where should I stay in Colorado Springs for hiking?

The Westside (near Old Colorado City and Manitou Springs) and the Broadmoor/Cheyenne Cañon area are the two best options. Both put you minutes away from world-class trailheads. The Westside is closer to Garden of the Gods and Red Rock Canyon, while the Broadmoor area offers immediate access to the beautiful North and South Cheyenne Cañons.
