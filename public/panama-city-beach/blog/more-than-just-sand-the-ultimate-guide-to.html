More Than Just Sand: The Ultimate Guide to Panama City Beach Adventures

2026-07-18 • 9 min read
Home / Panama City Beach Stays / More Than Just Sand: The Ultimate Guide to Panama City Beach Adventures

You already know about the 27 miles of sugar-white sand. That’s why you’re here, right? But what about the days you want to shake the sand from your toes and find a different kind of adventure? That’s where the real magic of Panama City Beach lies—in its sprawling nature preserves, bustling entertainment districts, and quiet, historic corners that most tourists fly right past.

Forget the generic tourist traps. This is your guide to experiencing the other side of PCB, the one that’s packed with hiking, shopping, wildlife encounters, and local flavor. Let’s dive into the incredible things to do in Panama City Beach when you’re ready for a break from the beach.

Embrace the Wild at St. Andrews State Park

Sitting at the far eastern end of Panama City Beach, St. Andrews State Park is a 1,200-acre paradise that feels a world away from the main strip. It’s consistently ranked as one of the best state parks in the country, and for good reason. It’s not just a beach; it’s an ecosystem.

Address: 4607 State Park Ln, Panama City, FL 32408

Hiking and Bicycling Trails

The park has two main trails: the Heron Pond Trail, which takes you through a flatwood pine forest, and the Gator Lake Trail, offering scenic views of the lake. Keep your eyes peeled for alligators, deer, and a huge variety of coastal birds. The paved park roads are also perfect for a leisurely bike ride if you’d rather cruise than hike.

The Jetties: A Snorkeler’s Dream

This is the absolute best spot for easy-access snorkeling in PCB. The rock jetties create a calm, protected pool of emerald water teeming with marine life. You’ll see everything from colorful sheepshead and snapper to blue crabs skittering along the rocks.

  • Local Tip: The secret to avoiding the crowds is to go on a weekday morning. The water visibility is usually best before the afternoon boat traffic kicks up. Park in the lot closest to the jetties and the Gulf-side beach.

Kayaking in the Grand Lagoon

For a calmer water experience, rent a kayak or stand-up paddleboard from the park’s concession stand and explore the Grand Lagoon. It’s a fantastic way to see nesting shorebirds and get a different perspective of the park, away from the Gulf waves.

Conquer the Urban Oasis of Pier Park

If St. Andrews is PCB’s natural heart, Pier Park is its vibrant, beating pulse. Located on the west end of the beach, this massive outdoor complex is way more than just a shopping mall. It’s a destination in itself where you can easily spend an entire day and evening.

Address: 600 Pier Park Dr, Panama City Beach, FL 32413

Retail Therapy Done Right

From big names like Target and Dillard’s to surf shops and quirky boutiques like Ron Jon Surf Shop and Coastal Casuals, you can find anything you need here. It’s the perfect place to grab souvenirs that aren’t just cheap t-shirts, find a new swimsuit, or simply enjoy some air-conditioned browsing on a hot day.

Non-Stop Entertainment

Pier Park is packed with family-friendly fun. * The SkyWheel: Get the best views of the coastline from 200 feet up in a climate-controlled gondola. Go at sunset for a truly spectacular experience. * The Grand IMAX Theatre: Catch the latest blockbuster on a massive screen. This is a lifesaver on a rare rainy day. * Dave & Buster’s: A classic arcade and restaurant that’s fun for all ages.

Beyond the Food Court

While you can find quick bites, the dining at Pier Park is surprisingly diverse. To avoid the seafood focus of our other guides, let’s talk about something different. For one of the best burgers in town, head to The Craft Bar. Their gastropub menu is fantastic, but the “Craft Burger” with white cheddar and bacon jam is my go-to. Another solid choice is Oishi Thai Restaurant & Sushi Bar for when you’re craving something fresh and flavorful.

Choose Your Adventure: Conservation Park vs. Gulf World

Looking for a dose of nature or a wildlife encounter? PCB has two fantastic but very different options on the west end, just a short drive from each other.

For the Hiker: Conservation Park

This is a true local’s gem. With over 2,900 acres and 24 miles of trails, the Conservation Park is a haven for hikers, trail runners, and bird watchers. The trails range from a short 0.6-mile loop to a challenging 11-mile trek, all winding through cypress domes and pine forests on well-marked paths and boardwalks.

  • Address: 100 Conservation Dr, Panama City Beach, FL 32413
  • Local Tip: Bring bug spray, especially in the warmer months. The best time to go is early morning before the heat sets in, and you’ll have the trails mostly to yourself. Dogs are welcome on leashes!

For the Animal Lover: Gulf World Marine Park

If you want guaranteed wildlife sightings and educational shows, Gulf World is your spot. It’s a classic Florida attraction that’s been delighting families for generations. The dolphin and sea lion shows are fantastic, but the real highlight is the interactive programs. You can book experiences to swim with dolphins, interact with stingrays, or get up close with a sea lion. It’s a splurge, but an unforgettable one.

  • Address: 15412 Front Beach Rd, Panama City Beach, FL 32413

Go Off The Beaten Path: Local PCB Secrets

Ready to really explore like a local? Venture away from the main drag of Front Beach Road and you’ll be rewarded with some authentic Florida charm.

Explore Historic St. Andrews

Not to be confused with the state park, the neighborhood of St. Andrews is a historic fishing village on the bay side of Panama City. It has a completely different vibe—slower, quainter, and full of character. Stroll down Beck Avenue, pop into the unique art galleries, and grab a coffee at Amavida Coffee and Tea, a local roaster known for its fair-trade brews. On Saturdays, the Market at St. Andrews is a must-visit for local produce, crafts, and baked goods.

The Panama City Beach Winery

Yes, you read that right. A winery at the beach! This isn’t your typical vineyard experience. The Panama City Beach Winery specializes in wines made from 100% real fruit—think key lime, mango, and blackberry. They offer free tastings, and it’s a fun, quirky stop to pick up a unique souvenir.

  • Address: 8730 Thomas Dr #1103b, Panama City Beach, FL 32408

Frequently Asked Questions About PCB Activities

What is there to do in Panama City Beach besides the beach?

Plenty! You can explore nature preserves like St. Andrews State Park and Conservation Park for hiking and birdwatching, enjoy shopping and entertainment at Pier Park, visit Gulf World Marine Park for animal encounters, or explore the historic neighborhood of St. Andrews for a taste of Old Florida.

Is Pier Park in Panama City Beach worth visiting?

Absolutely. Pier Park is a massive outdoor lifestyle center with over 100 stores, numerous restaurants, a movie theater, and attractions like the SkyWheel. It’s a great destination for a full day of fun, especially if you’re looking for a break from the sun or a lively evening activity.

What is the best time of year to visit Panama City Beach for outdoor activities?

For activities like hiking and biking, the best times are spring (March-May) and fall (September-November). The weather is cooler and less humid, and the crowds are smaller than in the peak summer season. However, water activities like snorkeling are best from late spring through early fall when the Gulf water is warm.

Can you walk around Panama City Beach?

While you can walk to nearby restaurants and shops depending on where you’re staying, PCB is quite spread out. The main strip, Front Beach Road, is very long. To explore attractions like St. Andrews State Park, Pier Park, and Conservation Park, which are miles apart, you will definitely need a car.

Panama City Beach is so much more than its world-famous coastline. It’s a destination brimming with diverse adventures waiting just beyond the dunes. From serene nature trails to bustling entertainment hubs, there’s a whole other side of PCB to discover.
